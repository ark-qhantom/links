About Qhantom

Qonverging the human experience.

Qhantom is a technology and worldbuilding company bringing storytelling, art, research and human collaboration together. We believe technology should expand what people can create and experience together, without losing the imagination, care and individuality that make those experiences meaningful.

Our story goes like this, Qhantom comes from another dimension. After accidentally discovering Earth, its team establishes a base in Antarctica and begins working toward a stable connection with their home realm, Qigana. Their culture, stories and technologies become a bridge between worlds.

Here on Earth, that same idea guides our work. Realm Runners is our original universe, told through animation, music, lore and interactive experiences. Qonvergence explores new ways for people, ideas and worlds to come together, including our early research into persistent digital worlds.

Different mediums, and one shared ambition: more connection, more collaboration and more possibility for the human experience.
