About Qhantom

Qhantom (Phan-tom) makes the future feel inhabitable: trusted technology, broadcast worlds, and sound, so people keep meaning, agency, and home inside intelligent systems.

Qhantom is organized around a single question: how do humans find meaning, agency, continuity, connection, and home inside increasingly vast intelligent systems?

Qonvergence In development
(Con-ver-gence) Brings people, ideas and worlds together. Research continues beneath it.
Realm Runners
Follows travelers and their missions across the realms, with their stories produced for Earth by Qhantom.
Sonics
Brings together music, artists, and sound from those worlds.

Careers

Qhantom is building an alternate path for the AI era: cinematic worlds, creative tools, and technology that still feels human.

We are looking for engineers, artists, writers, and builders who want to help turn that world into something real.

These are early signals for the kinds of people Qhantom needs. Some roles may become founding paths; others may begin as project-based collaborations. Every role is open to interns.

How to apply

Email careers@qhantom.com with:

  • The role and a short introduction
  • A portfolio, reel, GitHub, or other work
  • Why you want to help build Qhantom

Curiosity, taste, and genuine interest matter more here than traditional credentials. If you believe you should be building with us, send your work anyway.
